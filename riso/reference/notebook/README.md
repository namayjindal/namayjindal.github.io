## 2 — namay.xyz vision: **what is it for?**

- just to have a space that is mine. kinda performative but also a digital space where
  i can try out different design strategies and see what works.
- i also am so scared of putting my thoughts online. this could be my own space.
  no need to market or anything. but if someone cares, they could find it. i want to
  showcase an opinionated personality. a bold one. and feel more confident about myself
  through this. put something out there that i'm proud of.

### what does the actual content look like?

- **not tech centric.** i want to position myself as a creative who happens to be in tech.
- definitely a lot more design engineer who is also really good at data/product, and goes
  out of his way to think critically about things outside of the daily routine of my job.
- i want the website to be kinda what **'elate'** was.
  - a landing page. **each section of the website is named, like a room you enter.**
  - elate had 'synesthesia' for all media, 'the fine print' for anything editorial etc.

Key notes on the sketch:

- the logo gets its own **hover animation**, and the story behind it (made it at 14/15)
  is worth telling on the page. It came out of Japanese/Asian type — compact, edgy,
  nightclub.
- the softboard is the hero. post-its are the navigation; **clicking one takes you to
  that section.**
- *"inspired by cool japanese/asian types and their compactness, very edgy nightclub
  vibe."* **This describes the logo**, not the site's body typography — it is the register
  the mark itself came out of.
- below the fold is a **scroll page**, not a second screen: a big random riso fruit
  illustration (possibly a small game), then snippet rows for **media**, **tinkerings**
  and **writings**, each with a "see more →".
